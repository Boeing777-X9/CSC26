## Platform Objectives

The Cyberspace Club platform provides a centralized hub for:
* **Event Management**: Community workshops, hackathons, and technical sessions.
* **Member Portal**: Member registration, profile management, and role-based permissions.
* **Certificate Issuance & Verification**: Tamper-proof achievement certificates for event attendees.
* **Media Gallery**: High-resolution event photos and highlights.
* **Newsletter Subscriptions**: Community updates and technical announcements.
